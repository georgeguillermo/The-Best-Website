LIMBUS COMPANY

What is Limbus Company?

Limbus Company is, in my opinion, THE best gacha game available to play right now.

What is it about?

Before getting into anything about Limbus is set in the Project Moon Universe in a place ominously reffered to as "The City". The City is split into 26 districts, each one corresponding to a different letter of the alphabet and each district is split into 2 different sections, the backstreets, which is basically the slums of a district, and the wings, whose name is symbolic for how the leading corporation in the District protects the people in here.

Anyways, enough rambling, onto the story of Limbus. Limbus company is set after Library of Ruina and Lobotomy Corporation, the two previous project moon games, and it follows a group of 15 people, comprised of 13 "sinners", 1 guide, and 1 bus driver. The reason why you dont see the 10th sinner, Dante, is because they went crazy at the beginning of the game, having to have Faust, Sinner 2, put a prosthetic on their head so that they would go back to normal, and this is where you come in. YOU play as Dante as they manage the other sinners in combat.

Not to brag, but I have 450 hours in Limbus Company on Steam. Also the main band behind the music in Limbus Company is called Mili. I have a lot of their songs on my playlist because of how good they are. My personal favorite is a tie between "Hero" and "Rendezvous". Both of them are played at the climax at their respective cantos.
